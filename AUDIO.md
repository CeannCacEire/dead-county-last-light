# Audio settings

Open **Settings, Audio**. Sound only starts after your first tap or key press, as browsers require.

## Mixer

| Slider | Controls |
| --- | --- |
| Master volume | Everything |
| Music | Dynamic music layers |
| Effects | Footsteps, items, combat, doors, vehicles |
| Ambience and weather | Wind, rain, forest, town, night, indoor rooms |
| Interface | Menu and button sounds |
| Mute all audio | Silences everything |
| Low-power audio mode | Fewer simultaneous voices and lighter layers for older phones |

Voice, weather and creature sounds follow the effects and ambience levels. The game is fully playable without sound: every important event also has a visual cue and a message.

## How it works

- **Footsteps** depend on the surface: grass, dirt, mud, concrete, pavement, gravel, wood, metal and water. They change for walking, sprinting, crouching, injured and heavily loaded movement.
- **Positional sound** uses distance falloff and stereo panning. Walls muffle sounds on the other side. Interiors and outdoors use different reverb.
- **Creatures** groan, shuffle and react to noise. Friendly animals have their own calls.
- **Music** is built from layers that fade in and out smoothly: calm, tension, danger, large group, night, safehouse, menu and victory.
- **Limits** keep the mix clear: priority-based voice limits, throttling of repeated sounds, and pooled sources.
- Audio pauses when you pause the game and when the tab is hidden, and resumes when you return.
