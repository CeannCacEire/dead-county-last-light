# Changelog

## 1.0

Complete visual, audio and interface overhaul. Gameplay, survival rules, save format and controls on desktop are unchanged, and existing saves still load.

### Graphics
- New illustrated survival look with a muted palette and clear silhouettes.
- New sprites for the player, survivors, infected and animals, with walk, sprint and crouch animation.
- Redrawn terrain, forests, grass, rocks, shrubs, crops, fences, roads, parking, vehicles, signs, litter, walls, windows, doors, furniture, containers, tools, weapons and buildings.
- Biome blending, tile variation and ground decals.
- Door states (open, closed, locked, damaged, barricaded) and breakable windows.
- Separately lit interiors, street lights, lamps, flashlights, headlights, candles, emergency lights and generators.
- Day and night grading, fog, rain, puddles, dust, smoke, sparks, leaves and foliage sway.
- Smooth camera follow, zoom (pinch and wheel), shake and vignettes for danger, panic, low health, fatigue and darkness.
- Quality levels Performance, Low, Medium and High, with automatic adjustment and per-effect reductions.

### Sound
- New audio engine with music, ambience, weather, effects, interface, voice and creature buses.
- Surface-aware footsteps with injured, sprint, crouch and loaded variants.
- Positional audio with falloff, panning, wall muffling and indoor and outdoor reverb.
- Eight music layers with smooth transitions, plus creature and animal voices that react to noise.
- Master, music, effects, ambience and interface sliders, full mute, and a low-power mode.

### Interface
- New title screen, menus, settings (Graphics, Audio, Controls, Accessibility, Game), pause, journal, map, death and victory screens.
- New mobile layout with floating joystick, right-side aim, Act, Attack, Search, Sprint, Crouch, Pack, Map, Light, Heal and Pause buttons.
- Left-handed layout, button editor (move, resize, hide), opacity, portrait and landscape layouts.
- New HUD with icon meters, objective, compass, minimap, noise and visibility meters, carry weight, and weather and time.
- HUD options: compact, high contrast, colour-blind friendly, larger text, reduced animation.
- Inventory: thumbnails, search, category filters, sorting, hold-to-split, desktop drag and drop, capacity feedback, equipment slots including gloves and scarf.
- Crafting: recipe cards with material counts, search and a ready-only filter.

### Other
- Added Work Gloves and Wool Scarf.
- Wording and metadata cleaned across the page, menus, documentation and code.
